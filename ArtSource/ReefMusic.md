# Deep Reef Loop

## Current battle selection

The approved battle tracks are `A_HeavyCombat.wav`, `B_RushCombat.wav`, and `C_DeepDuel.wav`, generated by `battle_music_variants_v2.py` and copied unchanged into `Assets/Mantis/Audio`. `LoadBattleTracks` imports them as stereo PCM streaming clips. The original ambient composition below remains available but is no longer assigned to the duel.

Each `NextRound` (including reset/rematch) draws uniformly from the three tracks using an independent random generator. Repeats are allowed. The selected clip starts at the beginning, fades in, and loops until the next round; gameplay slow motion does not change playback speed. M mute and impact ducking are preserved.

## Earlier ambient composition

Original procedural composition for the mantis shrimp duel, generated by `Assets/Mantis/Scripts/Editor/ReefMusicBuilder.cs`.

- 88 BPM, 4/4, 16 bars (43.636 seconds), stereo 44.1 kHz PCM.
- D minor color: Dm(add9), Bbmaj9, Gm9, Asus4/minor seventh voicings, each lasting two bars; the second pass changes the sparse melodic motif.
- Slowly swelling pads, short bass plucks, a low heartbeat pulse, wooden taps, distant FM notes and filtered water noise.
- All event tails and stereo delays wrap around the file boundary. There is no silence inserted between repeats.
- Build output: `Assets/Mantis/Audio/DeepReefLoop.wav`. Rebuilt with the duel build command.
- Runtime `ReefMusic` fades in, keeps music independent of combat slow motion and round resets, and briefly lowers the music at major impacts. M toggles music without muting effects.
- No external recordings, sampled songs or third-party music assets are used.

The editor check verifies loop-boundary continuity and peak level. Runtime checks verify playback across resets and beyond one complete loop. Speaker/headset listening and final mix balance still require user evaluation.
